Thank you for choosing **Sakinah**. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how your data is protected when using the Sakinah mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

#### A. Location Data (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)
- **Purpose:** Sakinah requests access to your device's approximate and precise location strictly to:
  1. Calculate accurate Islamic prayer times (*Namoz vaqtlari*) based on your exact geographical coordinates.
  2. Determine the correct Qibla compass direction (*Qibla yo'nalishi*).
- **Processing & Storage:** Your location data is calculated locally on your device. **We do not track, collect, store on external servers, or share your location data with any third parties.**

#### B. Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)
- **Purpose:** Sakinah requests notification permission to deliver timely prayer time alerts (*Azan/Namoz eslatmalari*) and daily Quran reminders.
- **Control:** You can turn notifications on or off at any time within your device's system settings.

#### C. Foreground Service & Media Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`)
- **Purpose:** This service is used exclusively to allow continuous Quran audio recitation playback while the app is in the background or when the device screen is locked.
- **Usage:** It does not collect any personal data and terminates when audio playback stops.

#### D. Device Storage & Local Data
- **Purpose:** The app stores your user preferences (such as selected language, recitation audio downloads, reading bookmarks, and Hifz memorization progress) locally on your device storage.
- **Processing:** This data remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to any remote server.

---

### 2. Microphone & Audio Recording Notice
- Sakinah **does not record audio** or access your device's microphone. All audio features are strictly for playback of Quran recitations.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
Sakinah may interact with standard third-party services necessary for core functionalities:
- **Audio Delivery Providers:** Public CDNs used solely to stream or download Quran recitations.
- **Expo Framework:** Provides native runtime environment and crash diagnostics.

These providers do not receive any personally identifiable information (PII) from Sakinah.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Sakinah does not address anyone under the age of 13 for personal data collection. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

---

### 5. Data Security
We prioritize the security of your data. Because all personal settings, bookmarks, and location calculations remain locally on your device, your personal data is protected by your device's own security features.
